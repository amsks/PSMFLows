## What this says

**15,000 evaluation episodes, zero successes.** Every one of the 30 cells is 0/500, so every
Wilson interval is [0.000, 0.008] and the pooled 300k–500k mean is 0.000 ± 0.000. This is not
a noisy zero: it is the absence of a single success anywhere in the ladder.

The in-loop 50-episode evals agree — `evaluation/success` is `0.0` at **every** logged point
(step 1 and each 50k thereafter) on all three seeds. On cube the in-loop eval at least tracked
the 500-episode ladder loosely; here there is nothing for it to track.

**No figure accompanies this table.** A plot of 30 identical zeros against 33 identical
in-loop zeros carries no information the table does not, and drawing a "learning curve" that
is a flat line at the axis would misrepresent a structural null as a measurement.

### The result is CONFOUNDED by the discount — read it with that caveat first

**This ladder was run at the repo default `discount=0.98`, an effective horizon of ~50 steps,
on a maze with a 1000-step episode limit.** Later the same session, the antmaze H2 test
(HANDOFF 2026-09-06) showed that this exact setting — not the environment — was what pinned
antmaze at the BC floor: retraining antmaze at `gamma=0.995` (horizon 200) moved it from
0.081 to **0.519 ± 0.175** over three seeds, with every seed's Wilson interval excluding BC.

Pointmaze-medium is the same shape of problem: a 1000-step navigation episode whose goal is
hundreds of steps away. A value function blind past ~50 steps cannot steer toward it. So
**the honest reading of this table is "the affine arm scores 0.000 on pointmaze at
gamma=0.98", not "pointmaze is closed to this method"** — the horizon confound has to be
removed before the stronger claim is available. A `gamma=0.995` retrain is the direct test
and has been launched (see the HANDOFF entry).

### The prior standing before this run, which is now only one of two live explanations

`docs/COMPENDIUM.md` §4.11 ("the Rung-1 root cause, closed, do not re-audit") recorded that
all Stage-C variants read 0.0 on pointmaze task1, with a cause that is **not a bug**:

1. Exhaustive reachability over a 13x13 grid of the `[-3,3]^2` latent box plus 64 dataset/goal
   preimages, each rolled for two full 1000-step episodes: **0 of 233 latents ever reach the
   goal**, and 75% never get within ~23.5 in a maze ~30 across.
2. The expert's route is latent white noise -- within-episode preimage variance / marginal
   variance = **0.99**. The BC flow factorises behaviour as (state -> conditional, u ->
   quantile) and the expert's direction choice is driven by a goal that is *not in the
   observation*, so that variance is forced into `u` independently at every step. **Routes
   exist only as latent sequences**, never as a persistent policy index -- Assumption
   *coherence* failing empirically.

Two things keep that from settling the present table. First, the reachability probe rolled
**fixed-`u`** policies, whereas this arm acts by `gpi`, re-selecting `u` from 64 prior draws
*every step*; that is a strictly larger policy family. Second, whether the critic can *steer*
that per-step choice toward a distant goal is precisely what the discount controls -- so the
coherence mechanism and the horizon mechanism are not alternatives, and this run cannot
separate them. The affine psi head -- the change that moved cube from 0.083 to a 0.415 late
mean -- moves pointmaze not at all *at this horizon*.

The BC control is itself at the floor, so pointmaze does not even offer a bar to clear: the
frozen behaviour flow acting alone solves this task once in 500 episodes. Quoting the agent as
"below BC" would over-read a 1-episode difference. The honest statement is that **both the
method and its control are at zero on this environment**, and the ceiling is a property of the
Stage-A/B latent construction, not of the Stage-C representation.

### Status

Pointmaze at `gamma=0.98` is a **third environment with no result**, but after the antmaze H2
finding it may NOT be quoted as a structural null: the one variable now known to have
manufactured a false null on a sibling 1000-step maze was held at the value that caused it.
Of the three environments with published Stage-A/B artifacts the standing is: cube works
(d_a=5, 0.415 late mean); antmaze works once the horizon is fixed (d_a=8, 0.519 at
gamma=0.995); pointmaze is **untested at an adequate horizon**. See
`docs/plans/2026-09-06-env-roadmap.md` -- and note that its recommendation to avoid further
navigation envs was written before H2 and should be re-read in that light.
