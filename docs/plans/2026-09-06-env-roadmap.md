# Environment roadmap — what the paper promises, what is covered, what each new env costs

Date: 2026-09-06. Branch `feat/inversion-integration`. Machine: KISSKI (SLURM/H100).

Written because the affine paper-strict `psmflow` (repo default) is a real result on exactly
one environment — cube, late mean 0.415 vs BC 0.072 — and null on the second, antmaze
(0.081 vs 0.072). This document says which environment to add next, what it costs, and what
the paper actually obliges us to show.

> **AMENDED 2026-09-07 — read this first.** This document was written before the antmaze H2
> result. H2 showed that antmaze's null was **not** the environment but `discount=0.98`, a
> ~50-step effective horizon on a 1000-step maze: retrained at `gamma=0.995` antmaze scores
> **0.519 ± 0.175** over three seeds against BC 0.072. Two conclusions below are therefore
> superseded: (a) "antmaze is null" — it is not, once the horizon is fixed; (b) the advice to
> avoid navigation envs, which rested on that null. The pointmaze ladder run since (all 30
> cells 0.000, `docs/tables/affine_pointmaze_ladder.md`) was itself at `gamma=0.98` and is
> horizon-confounded for the same reason; H3 (`docs/design/2026-09-07-discount-sweep.md`)
> is retraining cube and pointmaze at `gamma=0.99` to separate it. **The d_a hypothesis below
> is also weakened**: antmaze is d_a=8 and works at an adequate horizon, so d_a=5 vs 8 is no
> longer the leading explanation of the cube/antmaze split — the horizon is. The cost tables,
> the code-change inventory, the eval/task-id analysis and the dataset-mirror section are
> unaffected and remain correct.

## 1. What the paper text and tables promise

**The ICLR draft names no environment at all.** `PAPER/ICLR/content/experiments.tex` and
`content/related-work.tex` are both **0 bytes**. Every environment commitment in the prose is
generic:

| where | what it says |
|---|---|
| `PAPER/ICLR/main.tex:113` | "larger-scale (**OGBench**) experiments that show that we can select policies which remain in the data support" |
| `PAPER/ICLR/main.tex:115` | "Show that we can get zero- and few-shot value training on **goal reaching tasks in OGBench**" |
| `PAPER/ICLR/main.tex:123` | "Experimental evidence will be limited to **ogbench**, although we could go for something cooler if we don't make ICLR." |
| `PAPER/ICLR/content/introduction.tex:27` | "We implement and validate our technique on the **OGBench tasks**" |
| `PAPER/ICLR/content/introduction.tex:28` | `We show ... \todo[inline]{add results here}` |

**No DMC, no ExORL, no D4RL anywhere in `PAPER/`** — not in the `.tex`, not cited from
`bib/local.bib`. `park2024ogbench` (`bib/local.bib:314`) is present but uncited.

So the paper is committed to *the OGBench family* and to nothing more specific. That is a
freedom, not a gap: we choose the envs, and the only real obligation is that "OGBench" be
represented by more than one working environment.

### Envs with numbers in the generated tables

`PAPER/ICLR/tables/table_headline.tex` is generated by `tools/make_tables.py` and names only:

- **cube-single-play** (`:26-28` preimage arms, `:33-47` affine ladder, `:58-68` GPI ablations)
- **antmaze-medium-navigate** (`:29-32`, `:48-57`)

Most rows read `--` (no eval JSON). `PAPER/ICLR/tables/table_fraction.tex` is **entirely `--`**
and is cube-only by construction (`tools/make_tables.py:258-278`).
`docs/tables/results.md:3` states the scope: "cube-single-play-singletask-v0".

**pointmaze appears in no paper table.**

### `docs/COMPENDIUM.md` status of the three published envs

| env | status |
|---|---|
| `cube-single-play` | **Primary.** Affine-strict 0.415 late mean vs BC 0.072. Arm is *not converged* — ±0.4 swings between 50k checkpoints on 2 of 3 seeds (HANDOFF 09-06). |
| `antmaze-medium-navigate` | **Null.** "at or below the BC floor" (`:25`). Inversion pathology: ESS 8.8→18.8/200, coverage 0.016→0.064, 881/1M invalid preimages (`:507-511`). |
| `pointmaze-medium-navigate` | **Settled negative, "closed, do not re-audit"** (`:515-533`). All Stage-C variants read 0.0. Cause is not a bug: 0 of 233 latents ever reach the goal; within-episode preimage variance / marginal variance = 0.99, i.e. routes exist only as latent *sequences*, not as a persistent index `u`. |

## 2. The eval evaluates ONE OGBench task, not all five

This is the most consequential finding for planning, so it is stated with the code.

`tools/eval_checkpoint.py:199` builds a single env from a single name:

```python
env, eval_env, train_dataset, _ = make_env_and_datasets(cfg.env_name, frame_stack=cfg.frame_stack)
```

and `:240` hands it to `evaluate(...)`. Inside `utils/evaluation.py:86,93` the resets are
`env.reset(seed=seed)` and `observation, info = env.reset()` — **no `options={'task_id': ...}`
is ever passed**, so the task is whatever the env id baked in. `envs/env_utils.py:137-142`
just forwards the name to `ogbench.make_env_and_datasets`.

**Suffix → task id.** `ogbench/utils.py:154-161` splits the name: `...-singletask-taskN-v0`
becomes gym id `...-singletask-taskN-v0` with the *dataset type token dropped*, and the
dataset file is the same `<env>-v0.npz` for every task id. Only the **relabeled rewards**
differ (`ogbench/utils.py:199-202`). Every OGBench env registers six ids — bare plus
`task1..task5` (`ogbench/manipspace/__init__.py:175-177`, `ogbench/locomaze/__init__.py:248-250`).

**Default task when the suffix is bare** (`reward_task_id == 0`):

| family | default | code |
|---|---|---|
| point/ant/humanoid maze | **task 1** | `ogbench/locomaze/maze.py:348-349` |
| antsoccer | task 4 | `ogbench/locomaze/maze.py:586-587` |
| all `cube-*` | **task 2** | `ogbench/manipspace/envs/cube_env.py:359-360` |
| `scene` | task 2 | `ogbench/manipspace/envs/scene_env.py:141-142` |
| `puzzle-3x3/4x4` | task 4 | `ogbench/manipspace/envs/puzzle_env.py:450-455` |
| `puzzle-4x5/4x6` | task 2 | `ogbench/manipspace/envs/puzzle_env.py:456-459` |

So this project's headline numbers are: cube **task 2**, antmaze **task 1**, pointmaze
**task 1** (`scripts/eval500.sh:50,54,58`). OGBench's own protocol reports all five
(`docs/README-fql.md:131-136`).

### The consequence: 4 more tasks per env are nearly free

`agents/psmflow.py` reads `batch['rewards']` **only** in `infer_z` / `infer_z_a` /
`infer_eval_z` (`:609-628`) — the eval-time closed-form task-vector inference. **No training
loss touches rewards.** And `tools/eval_checkpoint.py` loads no preimage npz and never runs
`main.py`'s pairing guard (that guard is `main.py:85-168`).

⇒ **An existing cube checkpoint can be evaluated zero-shot on all five cube tasks by changing
`env_name` alone.** No retraining, no Stage A, no Stage B. This is the cheapest evidence in
the project and it is exactly what a zero-shot-RL paper should be showing.

## 3. What a genuinely new environment costs

### Per-env work

| stage | cost | notes |
|---|---|---|
| dataset | ~270 MB train + 27 MB val | **See §5 — the upstream host is currently 404.** |
| **A** behaviour flow | **~1–1.5 h**, 1 GPU | Only measured figure: pointmaze 500k = **1h27m** (`docs/HANDOFF.md:2688-2691`). Runs at `flow_steps=100`, which is why it beats FQL's 28–34 min. |
| **HPO** inversion knobs | **~1 h** (50 SMAC trials, strictly sequential) | 65 s/trial at d_a=5, 127 s at d_a=8 (`docs/HANDOFF.md:1923`). `tools/hpo_preimage_inversion.py`. Do NOT take α/prior_scale/n_steps from `configs/inversion/default.yaml`'s coarse frontier. |
| **B** inversion, 1M rows | **~10–25 h**, 1 GPU | 10 h measured on cube at the original settings (`HANDOFF:2792`); 23 h at n_steps=12/N=200, 33 h at n_steps=17 (`HANDOFF:1526-1528`); ~13–20 h projected/booked for antmaze. Cost ≈ linear in `num_samples` × `n_steps` × rows. sbatch wall defaults to 36 h — raise, don't lose it. |
| **C** representation | **~2.7 h/seed** cube-class, **~4.1 h/seed** antmaze-class | Affine ψ measured: cube 2h41m, antmaze 4h05m (`HANDOFF:132`). One seed per GPU — two on one device is ~2× slower each. ×3 seeds. |
| eval ladder | 10 ckpt × 3 seeds × (4 min … 93 min) | The affine/GPI arm is the slow one: 10–11 s/episode ⇒ ~1.5 h per 500-ep cell. Budget 3 h wall per eval job. |
| BC control | 1 × 500-ep eval | Mandatory beside every number. |

**Total for one new env: ~1.5 h (A) + 1 h (HPO) + ~20 h (B) + ~8 h (C, 3 seeds parallel) +
~15 h of eval (parallel) ≈ 1.5–2 days wall clock with 3–10 GPUs, of which ~20 h is the
single-GPU Stage-B critical path.**

### Code changes required

**None in Python.** `main.py`, `tools/precompute_preimages.py`, `tools/eval_checkpoint.py`,
`tools/hpo_preimage_inversion.py`, `envs/env_utils.py:137-147` and all of `configs/` select the
env purely through `env_name=<...>` on the Hydra CLI.

What *does* need editing is a hardcoded `cube|antmaze|pointmaze` `case` block duplicated in
**nine** shell wrappers — each needs one new arm:

| file | lines |
|---|---|
| `scripts/slurm/precompute_preimages.sbatch` | 32, 41-46 |
| `scripts/slurm/train_psmflow.sbatch` | 51-53 |
| `scripts/slurm/eval500.sbatch` | 41-43 |
| `scripts/slurm/eval_gpi_ablation.sbatch` | 42-44 |
| `scripts/slurm/diag_flow_fit.sbatch` | 34-36 |
| `scripts/slurm/diag_gpi_selection.sbatch` | 38-40 |
| `scripts/eval500.sh` | 30, 48-61 |
| `scripts/run_recovery_tests.sh` | 36-41, 63-66 |
| `scripts/fetch_preimages_hf.sh` | 28-30 |

Stage B also needs `ALPHA`, `PRIOR_SCALE`, `N_STEPS` chosen per env by the HPO sweep. The
decode budget is d_a-dependent (`configs/hpo_preimage.yaml:14-16`: `0.1*sqrt(d_a)` → 0.22 at
d_a=5, 0.28 at d_a=8), so **cube's `hpo_decode_budget=0.22` transfers unchanged to every
manipulation env**, all of which are d_a=5.

## 4. Candidate environments

`d_a` is the variable that predicts whether Stage B works. All of `manipspace` shares one
5-D action space (`ogbench/manipspace/envs/manipspace_env.py:134-141`, no subclass overrides
it). Coverage falls off *geometrically* with d_a — the same α covers 0.74 of the prior at
d_a=2, 0.19 at d_a=5, and antmaze at d_a=8 is worse again
(`docs/TUNING_INVERSION.md:66-68`, `configs/inversion/default.yaml:18-26`).

## 5. Blocker: the OGBench dataset host is down

`https://rail.eecs.berkeley.edu/datasets/ogbench/<name>.npz` now **302s to
`https://iris.eecs.berkeley.edu/datasets/ogbench/<name>.npz`, which returns 404** (a
WordPress page). This affects *every* OGBench dataset, including ones we already hold — it is
an upstream move, not a proxy or network problem. `ogbench` 1.2.1 on PyPI and master on
GitHub still point at the same dead URL (`ogbench/utils.py:11`).

**Working route (verified 2026-09-06):** the community mirror `ryanhoangt/ogbench_data` on Hugging
Face carries the standard `<env>-v0.npz` / `<env>-v0-val.npz` files at the repo root.

```bash
.venv/bin/python - <<'EOF'
import os, shutil
from huggingface_hub import hf_hub_download
dst = os.path.expanduser('~/.ogbench/data')
for f in ['<env>-v0.npz', '<env>-v0-val.npz']:
    shutil.copyfile(hf_hub_download('ryanhoangt/ogbench_data', f, repo_type='dataset'), os.path.join(dst, f))
EOF
```

`ryanhoangt/ogbench_data` holds 171 files and was checked (2026-09-06) to carry **every
candidate in §4**: `scene-play-v0`, `cube-double-play-v0`, `cube-triple-play-v0`,
`cube-quadruple-play-v0`, `puzzle-3x3-play-v0`, `puzzle-4x4-play-v0` and all `pointmaze-*`,
each with its `-val` companion (plus the `-noisy` variants). So the host outage **blocks no
environment on this roadmap**. Other mirrors:
`zhouzypaul/ogbench_datasets` (45 files, flat layout), `yonghoon96/ogbench-mirror` (275 files,
the 10m/100m variants).

**A mirror needs no trust argument for our purposes:** `main.py:85-168` compares the env
dataset against the preimage npz (row count plus the first and last 1000 rows), so a wrong or
corrupted copy is refused at start-up rather than silently trained on.

Note also: **`OGBENCH_DATASET_DIR` is a no-op with ogbench 1.1.0.** `ogbench/utils.py:10`
hardcodes `DEFAULT_DATASET_DIR = '~/.ogbench/data'` and `envs/env_utils.py:139-140` never
passes `dataset_dir`. It works on this machine only because `$HOME/.ogbench/data` is the same
path the variable names. Put files in `~/.ogbench/data`.

## 6. Recommended order

### 0. First: five tasks per existing checkpoint — ~0 GPU-hours of training

Evaluate the three finished `affine_strict_cube` seeds on `cube-single-play-singletask-task1`
through `task5` instead of only the default task 2. Training is reward-free
(`agents/psmflow.py:609-628` is the only reader of `rewards`), so this is pure eval: 5 tasks ×
3 seeds × 1–2 late checkpoints, plus the matching BC control per task. It multiplies the
paper's evidence base by five without a single new Stage A or B, and it is the *zero-shot*
claim stated properly — one representation, five reward functions inferred in closed form.
Nothing else on this list has a comparable cost/benefit ratio. Do it before adding any env.

### 1. `scene-play` — the recommended next environment

- **d_a = 5, identical to cube**, the only regime where the method works. Coverage and ESS
  should land near cube's (112.9/200, cov 0.945 at α=20), not antmaze's (8.8/200, 0.016).
- **Dataset already on disk** (`scene-play-v0.npz`, 267 MB) — immune to the §5 host outage,
  which alone is a strong argument for it this week.
- **Manipulation, but a different task structure from cube**: a scene with a drawer, a window
  and buttons rather than a single block, so it tests generalisation of the claim rather than
  re-running it. d_obs 40 vs cube's 28.
- Reuses cube's `hpo_decode_budget=0.22` verbatim.

### 2. `cube-double-play` — strong second

Same d_a, dataset on disk (297 MB), and it is already `configs/config.yaml:11`'s default
`env_name`. Two blocks instead of one is the natural difficulty axis from the headline env.
Run it if scene-play works; it makes a three-env manipulation story.

### 3. `puzzle-3x3-play`

Third manipulation env, d_a=5, needs a download (check the mirror first). Only worth it once
1 and 2 are in.

### Explicitly not next

- ~~**antmaze-large-navigate** (d_a=8)~~ — **this bullet is superseded.** It argued that
  antmaze-medium was null from coverage collapse at d_a=8, so antmaze-large would buy a
  second null. H2 refuted the premise: antmaze-medium reaches 0.519 at `gamma=0.995`, so the
  inversion coverage figures (0.016→0.064) were never what capped it. antmaze-large is now a
  *reasonable* candidate — dataset already on disk, Stage A/B needed — though it should wait
  until H3 reports, since a larger maze needs at least as long a horizon.
- **humanoidmaze** (d_a=21). Stage B coverage falls geometrically with d_a; this is hopeless
  before it starts.
- **Any navigation env at all**, until the cube/antmaze split is explained. pointmaze is a
  diagnosed structural failure of Assumption *coherence* (routes are latent *sequences*, not
  a persistent index) and antmaze is null. Navigation is where this method does not work.

### The scientific bonus of going d_a=5

The Jacobian probe already refuted "local injectivity explains the cube/antmaze split"
(`COMPENDIUM:473-483`, `:591`). The two surviving candidate explanations are **d_a (5 vs 8)**
and **action norm (0.87 vs 1.99)**. A second and third *manipulation* env at d_a=5 is
therefore a test of what *was* the leading hypothesis. **Amended:** H2 has since made the
horizon, not d_a, the best explanation of the cube/antmaze split — antmaze is d_a=8 and works
at `gamma=0.995`. A d_a=5 expansion is still the right next step, but for the plainer reason
that it is the regime with a confirmed result and needs no new hypothesis; it is no longer a
crucial experiment. Any new env must be run at a horizon matched to its episode length, and
the per-env checklist now includes choosing `discount` from the episode limit rather than
inheriting 0.98.
