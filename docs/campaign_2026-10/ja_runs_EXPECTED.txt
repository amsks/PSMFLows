Joint actor runs, cube, written 2026-10-05 before launch.

Setup. Goal-conditioned measure M = phi(s,u,s+)^T h(g) + b(s,u,s+), softmax loss, hindsight
goals only (geometric, goal_random_frac 0, goal_cur_frac 0), as psmgoal_db_sm_cube, but the
bootstrap latent at s' is the actor's move at (s', g) instead of the data's next latent. The
actor is trained at a uniform future goal (fb_goals) on the shaped value (kernel-weighted
softmax measure over the batch's next states) or on the point value M(s,u,g).

Arms (3 seeds each, 500k steps, checkpoints 250k and 500k):
  psmgoal_ja_fbc3_sh_cube  flowbc actor, fb_bc_coeff 3.0, shaped value
  psmgoal_ja_fbc0_sh_cube  flowbc actor, fb_bc_coeff 0.0, shaped value
  psmgoal_ja_dsrl_sh_cube  dsrl actor (tanh-Gaussian, no BC term, learned alpha), shaped value
  psmgoal_ja_fbc3_pt_cube  flowbc actor, fb_bc_coeff 3.0, point value
Readouts: hgoal_each (best of 64 prior draws by M, each goal at its own h(g)) and actor_rel
(the actor fed one rewarding dataset state), five tasks, 500 episodes each.

Reference numbers: data bootstrap psmgoal_db_sm_cube hgoal_each 0.68 (1 seed so far),
squared 0.60. BC control 0.111.

Expected outcomes:
1. fbc3_sh: hgoal_each >= 0.68 five-task mean. actor_rel within 0.05 of its hgoal_each.
   Actor |u| mean about 0.8 per dim (a prior draw's |u| mean), not 0.15 (the earlier tanh
   actor pinned near u = 0 by the data-latent anchor).
2. fbc0_sh and dsrl_sh: below fbc3_sh on both readouts. Risk of collapse: the actor chases a
   bad early M and the bootstrap follows it. A collapse shows as hgoal_each at or below BC
   (0.111) with the actor's Q rising while m_softmax_diag falls.
3. fbc3_pt: below fbc3_sh. The point value at a far goal is flat in u.
4. If every arm sits at or below the data bootstrap (0.68) with three seeds agreeing, the
   actor in the bootstrap does not improve on the data's own next move on cube.

Logged per step (train.csv): actor_u_absmean, actor_delta_norm / actor_bc_err (flowbc),
actor_logp and actor_alpha (dsrl), actor_q, actor_p_own (softmax weight on the row's own
next state).
