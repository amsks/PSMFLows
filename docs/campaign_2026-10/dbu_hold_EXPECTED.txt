psmgoal data-bootstrap ablations: uniform hindsight goals (training) and the held gpi latent (eval).
Written 2026-10-04 before any job of this batch was submitted.
Extends docs/design/2026-10-03-psmgoal-data-bootstrap.md (groups psmgoal_db_{sq,sm}_cube).

A. Training groups: psmgoal_dbu_sq_cube, psmgoal_dbu_sm_cube. Seeds 0 1 2, 500000 steps,
   checkpoints 250000 and 500000, --time=16:00:00.
   Overrides = the db launches plus agent.goal_sampling=uniform:
     agent.policy_index=goal agent.bootstrap_source=data agent.train_actor=false
     agent.goal_random_frac=0.0 agent.goal_cur_frac=0.0 agent.measure_loss={squared|softmax}
     agent.goal_sampling=uniform
   Only difference from psmgoal_db_*_cube: the measure goal of row i is a uniform draw over the
   row's own s' and every later state of its trajectory (future_goal_idxs) instead of the
   geometric offset at goal_discount=0.98. The data bootstrap stays valid: from row i+1 the goal
   is again uniform over the remaining future.
   Eval: hgoal and hgoal_each at 250k and 500k; lp at 250k; five tasks, 500 episodes.
   BC control beside every number: cube 0.111.

B. Hold ablation on EXISTING checkpoints: psmgoal_db_sq_cube and psmgoal_db_sm_cube, seeds 0 1 2,
   step 250000, readout hgoal_each (agent.coef_source=hgoal_each agent.acting=gpi) with
   agent.gpi_hold=4 and agent.gpi_hold=8. The chosen latent is reused for H env steps, the
   action is decoded at the current state each step. gpi_hold=1 is the existing per-step argmax
   (the recorded hgoal_each numbers: sq sd000 250k tasks 1-5 = 0.562 0.552 0.584 0.616 0.614).
   60 eval500 jobs, names <group>_<sd>_250000_hgoal_each_hold<H>_task<t>.

Expected outcomes
- Uniform goals raise the cube softmax and squared hgoal / hgoal_each scores over the db groups:
  the test-time goals (rewarding states) are far from most rollout states, and the uniform draw
  trains h(g) and the measure on far goals about 10x more often than the geometric draw
  (goal_discount 0.98 puts most mass within 50 steps).
- hgoal_each at or above hgoal, as in the db groups.
- Hold H=4 on cube: about equal to or slightly above H=1 (the gpi argmax at 64 draws is noisy
  step to step; holding averages that noise over 4 steps without losing much reactivity).
- Hold H=8 on cube: below H=1 (pick-and-place needs direction changes within 8 steps).
- Antmaze would gain more from the hold (one ant move changes little), but antmaze is not in
  this launch.
- Failure that refutes A: dbu softmax hgoal at or below db softmax hgoal with three seeds agreeing.
- Failure that refutes B: hold4 below hold1 on both losses with three seeds agreeing.

Expected training signals (smoke)
- flags.json carries goal_sampling=uniform plus the data-bootstrap keys.
- No actor_* keys in train.csv; psm_loss finite; steps/second as the db runs (6 h 21 min for 500k).
