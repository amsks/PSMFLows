\begin{abstract}
Zero-shot reinforcement learning methods such as Forward--Backward representations (FB)
and Proto Successor Measures (PSM) learn task-agnostic occupancy representations from
reward-free data, but their training loops evaluate candidate actions that the dataset
never supports: value estimates at out-of-distribution actions are unconstrained, and the
policy-improvement argmax seeks out exactly those errors. We propose \emph{PSMFlows},
which removes this failure mode by construction. A behavior-cloned conditional flow
$G_\theta(s,u)$ maps latent noise to data-like actions; holding the noise fixed defines a
family of policies $\pi_u(s)=G_\theta(s,u)$ indexed by $u$, and a successor-measure
representation $\psi(s,u',u)^\top\varphi(x)$ is learned over this family, with dataset
transitions carrying their own latents via flow inversion. Every action the method
considers---during training and at inference---is a decode of the frozen flow, converting
the hard constraint $a\in\suppo\mu_\D(\cdot\mid s)$ into the benign constraint that
$\|u\|$ be typical. We describe the three-stage pipeline, a staged diagnostic protocol
that makes each assumption falsifiable in isolation, and report first results on OGBench:
the flow, inversion, and latent-family stages certify cleanly, while first
representation-learning runs isolate a concrete failure mode---the learned measure
discriminates policy indices without aligning them to realized behavior---that our
diagnostics localize and that motivates the next iteration of the training loss.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

% TODO(intro): full motivation + contributions. Placeholder summary for layout.
Behavior foundation models promise a single reward-free pretraining phase after which any
downstream reward can be solved zero-shot. The dominant family---FB
\citep{touati2021learning} and PSM \citep{agarwal2024protosm}---learns linear-algebraic
representations of successor measures. Applied to \emph{offline} data with limited
support, however, these methods inherit the classic offline-RL pathology: their Bellman
maximization queries actions the dataset cannot certify. Conservatism penalties (VC-FB;
\citealp{jeen2023conservative}) treat the symptom in the loss; we instead change the
policy space so the symptom cannot arise.

\textbf{Contributions.}
(i)~a formulation of zero-shot offline RL as successor-measure learning over a
\emph{flow-indexed} policy family, in which the training distribution has behavior-policy
concentrability $C=1$;
(ii)~an in-sample training algorithm in which dataset transitions carry their own latents
via flow inversion, with a relaxed-posterior (Laplace + importance-sampled EM) preimage
operator;
(iii)~a staged, falsifiable diagnostic protocol (D1--D4) that separates flow fidelity,
index coherence, inversion health, and control;
(iv)~an empirical study on OGBench, including a negative result whose diagnosis is, we
argue, itself informative about what successor-measure losses ground and what they do
not.

\section{Preliminaries}
\label{sec:prelim}

\textbf{Setting.} A reward-free MDP $(\mathcal S,\mathcal A,P,\gamma)$ with
$\mathcal A\subseteq\mathbb R^{d_a}$; an offline dataset
$\D=\{(s_i,a_i,s'_i)\}_{i=1}^N$ collected by an unknown behavior policy
$\mu(\cdot\mid s)$ with state marginal $\rho$. At test time a reward $r$ is revealed
(as labels on a sample of $\D$) and a policy must be produced with no further
interaction.

\textbf{Successor measures.} For a policy $\pi$,
$M^\pi(s,a,X)=\E\big[\sum_{t\ge1}\gamma^{t-1}\mathbf 1\{s_t\in X\}\mid s_0=s,a_0=a\big]$
is the discounted occupancy of $X\subseteq\mathcal S$. Then
$Q^\pi_r(s,a)=\int r\,dM^\pi(s,a,\cdot)$: one object serves every reward, which is the
basis of zero-shot RL. FB parameterizes $m^\pi(s,a,x)=F(s,a,z_\pi)^\top B(x)$;
PSM uses an affine decomposition $m^\pi = b + \Phi\, w_\pi$ whose policy dependence is
linear. Both train by a measure-matching temporal-difference loss and infer tasks by
projecting $r$ on the state basis.

\textbf{The limited-support problem.} The TD loss and the actor of these methods evaluate
$Q$ at actions from a training policy $\pi_{\mathrm{tr}}$. The relevant quantity is the
concentrability $C(\pi_{\mathrm{tr}})=\operatorname{ess\,sup}
\pi_{\mathrm{tr}}(a\mid s)/\mu(a\mid s)$: FB's deterministic actor gives $C=\infty$; PSM's
uniform action sampling gives $C$ finite only for full-support behavior. On play or
mode-restricted data neither holds, and value errors at unsupported actions are exactly
the ones the argmax selects. % TODO(prelim): cite offline-RL concentrability results.

\textbf{Conditional flow matching.} A velocity field $v_\theta(s,u_t,t)$ trained with the
CFM regression $\E\|v_\theta(s,(1-t)u_0+ta,t)-(a-u_0)\|^2$, $u_0\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$,
induces a decoder $G_\theta(s,\cdot)$: the time-one map of the ODE
$\dot u=v_\theta(s,u,t)$. With latent dimension equal to action dimension,
$G_\theta(s,\cdot)$ is a $C^1$ diffeomorphism of $\mathbb R^{d_a}$; its inverse
$E_\theta(s,\cdot)$ is computed by backward integration.

\section{Method: PSMFlows}
\label{sec:method}

\subsection{A flow-indexed policy family}
Fix the trained (and thereafter frozen) behavior flow. For $u\in\mathbb R^{d_a}$ define
the fixed-index policy $\pi_u(s)=G_\theta(s,u)$. Two properties make this family the
right search space for offline zero-shot RL. First, \emph{mass conversion}: for any set
$U$ of prior mass $1-\delta$, the decoded actions carry $1-\delta$ of the behavior mass
\emph{simultaneously at every state}, so ``stay on the data'' becomes ``keep $\|u\|$
typical''. Second, \emph{divergence isometry}: for any $\nu$ over latents,
$D_f\!\big(\pi_\nu \,\|\, \hat\mu_\theta\big)=D_f(\nu\,\|\,p_0)$ at every state, so
trust-region constraints against the (unknown) behavior density become closed-form
constraints against a standard normal. % TODO(method): restate formal versions + proofs (see technical report).

\subsection{Dataset latents by flow inversion}
Training a measure over $\{\pi_u\}$ requires transitions labeled with latents. Since
$G_\theta(s,\cdot)$ is invertible, each dataset action has a point preimage
$u^\star_i=E_\theta(s_i,a_i)$; because a whole basin of latents decodes near $a_i$, we
also fit the relaxed posterior
\begin{equation}
q_\alpha(u\mid s,a)\;\propto\;\mathcal N(u;0,I)\,
\exp\!\big(-\alpha\|G_\theta(s,u)-a\|\big),
\label{eq:posterior}
\end{equation}
by importance-sampled EM initialized from the Laplace approximation with covariance
$\propto (J^\top J)^{-1}$, $J=\partial G_\theta/\partial u$
(Figure~\ref{fig:preimage}). The temperature $\alpha$ trades sharpness against effective
sample size (ESS); both are measured and gated (\S\ref{sec:exp}). Under an exact flow,
$E_\theta(s,a)\sim\mathcal N(0,I)$ on data, so the $\chi^2_{d_a}$ typicality of
$\|u^\star\|^2$ is a direct, falsifiable test of the flow-equals-behavior assumption.

\subsection{Successor measures over the family}
We learn $m(s,u,u',x)=\psi(s,u',u)^\top\varphi(x)$, the density of
$M^{u\to u'}(s,\cdot)$: take the action indexed by $u$ now, then follow $\pi_{u'}$. The
Bellman identity closes on the diagonal,
$M^{u\to u'}(s,X)=\E_{s'}[\mathbf 1\{s'\in X\}+\gamma M^{u'\to u'}(s',X)]$, so a single
network suffices. The empirical loss is the standard measure-matching TD objective with
batch negatives, an orthonormality regularizer on $\varphi$, and three sampling rules:
the action slot $u$ is drawn from the stored preimage posterior of the transition
(clipped to the typical set); the index slot $u'$ is drawn fresh (mixed
$\mathcal N(0,I)$/permuted behavior latents); the bootstrap target is
$\bar\psi(s',u',u')$---the continuation latent \emph{is} the policy index. No decoded
action appears anywhere in training; under an exact flow the training distribution has
concentrability $C=1$ w.r.t.\ the behavior policy.

\subsection{Inference}
Given rewards on relabeled data, $w=\frac1N\sum_i r(s'_i)\varphi(s'_i)$ is the
least-squares task projection (exact under orthonormal $\varphi$), and
$\psi(s,u',u)^\top w$ estimates the corresponding $Q$. Sampled GPI over the fixed-index
family draws $K$ candidate latents and $M$ indices, takes
$\hat u=\arg\max_k\max_m\psi(s,u'_m,u_k)^\top w$, and acts $a=G_\theta(s,\hat u)$.
Section~\ref{sec:exp} shows this is bounded by what the family contains, which motivates
the variant below.

\subsection{LatentFlowPSM}
\label{sec:latentflowpsm}
The support construction does not require that a \emph{fixed} latent be the policy---only
that every executed action be a flow decode. LatentFlowPSM keeps the frozen decoder and
moves policy identity into the task vector. Learned are features $\varphi(x)$, successor
features $\psi(s,u,w)$ with $m(s,u,w,x)=\psi(s,u,w)^\top\varphi(x)$ and
$Q_w(s,u)=\psi(s,u,w)^\top w$, an actor $\pi_\eta(s,w,\epsilon)$ emitting a latent in the
clip box, and a flow-matching field over dataset latents used as the actor's
behavior-cloning anchor. The measure loss is the usual measure-matching objective with one
change: the continuation latent at $s'$ is the actor's, $u^{+}=\pi_\eta(s',w,\epsilon)$,
rather than a fixed index, so policy improvement happens in latent space while every
bootstrapped action remains a decode. The actor is trained by deterministic policy gradient
on $Q_w$ with the flow-matching anchor and ensemble pessimism; $\psi$ takes no actor
gradient and $G_\theta$ takes none at all. At deployment $w=\mathbb E_{\mathcal D}[r\varphi]$
and $a=G_\theta(s,\pi_\eta(s,w,\epsilon))$, with no test-time training.

\section{Experiments and current findings}
\label{sec:exp}

\textbf{Protocol.} OGBench \citep{park2024ogbench} \texttt{pointmaze-medium-navigate} and
\texttt{cube-single-play} (state-based, single-task rewards); FQL
\citep{park2025fql} as the per-task topline; in-repo, parity-verified PSM and FB as
zero-shot baselines, and \texttt{antmaze-medium-navigate} for the latent-actor results.
Headline numbers are 500-episode evaluations of the final checkpoint with Wilson 95\%
intervals; across-seed summaries are mean $\pm$ 95\% CI over $\ge3$ seeds. Every
zero-shot number is quoted beside the per-step-prior behavior-cloning control decoded from
the same frozen flow, since that is the baseline the method must beat. Gates D1--D4 run in
order, which localizes any failure to a single stage.

\textbf{Finding 1: the substrate works.} The behavior flow beats random controls by
1--3 orders of magnitude on distribution match (D1); fixed-latent policies are
reproducible and mutually distinct (D2); and at $\alpha{=}20$, $N{=}200$ the preimage
posterior passes every inversion gate (D3), with $99\%$ of inverted latents inside the
central $\chi^2_{d_a}$ typicality band---direct evidence for the exact-flow assumption at
the fidelity our gates demand.

\textbf{Finding 2: the temperature is measurable, and it matters.} At $\alpha{=}1$ the
Laplace proposal collapses to the prior (99.8\% of rows fully clipped), 39\% of posterior
means leave the typical set, and ESS $\approx$ 10; at $\alpha{=}20$ posterior means track
the $\chi_{d_a}$ radius and ESS exceeds the gate five-fold (Figure~\ref{fig:preimage}).
Latents computed at the wrong temperature are quantifiably noise.

\textbf{Finding 3 (negative, root-caused): the fixed-latent family has no goal-reaching
member.} Representation runs over the fixed-index family reach $0.00$ success on
pointmaze and $0.02$--$0.06$ on cube even with oracle rewards. This is not a training
failure: it is a property of the family. Rolling \emph{every} latent on a grid that covers
the whole box---plus dataset preimages and the preimages of goal-reaching
transitions---for full episodes, $0$ of $233$ candidates ever reach the pointmaze goal,
$2$ of $128$ on cube, $5$ of $64$ on antmaze (Figure~\ref{fig:reach}). The mechanism is
temporal, not per-state: at a fixed state the decoded action covers every direction
(angular circular variance $0.45$--$0.78$), but within an episode the inverted latents are
white (lag-1 autocorrelation $0.25$, decaying to zero by lag $50$; within-episode variance
is $0.99$ of the marginal). The behavior flow factorizes behavior into a state-conditional
and a per-step quantile, and the expert's route depends on a goal that is not in the
observation, so route identity is pushed into the latent \emph{sequence} and never into any
single $u$. Consequently every $\pi_u$ has the same value under any goal-reaching reward,
$\psi^\top w$ is flat over candidates ($\pm2.5\%$), and GPI's argmax drifts to the
saturated corners. The prediction this makes is quantitative and it holds: flow-GPI over
fixed $u$ scores $0.015$ on cube against a measured family ceiling of $2/128 = 0.0156$.
GPI was already extracting everything the family contained.

\textbf{Finding 4: moving the policy index into a task vector recovers most of the loss.}
The support construction survives Finding 3---every action is still a flow decode---but the
premise that a fixed noise vector \emph{is} a policy does not. We therefore keep the frozen
flow and make the index a task vector $w$, with an amortized actor $u_\eta(s,w)$ emitting a
latent per step and the measure backup bootstrapping the actor's latent at $s'$ rather than
a fixed index (\emph{LatentFlowPSM}, \S\ref{sec:method}). On cube-single this reaches
$0.236$ success ($500$ episodes/seed, $5$ seeds, Wilson $[0.219,0.253]$; across seeds
$0.236\pm0.071$) against a behavior-cloning control of $0.068$ $[0.049,0.093]$ measured
from the same frozen flow---a $3.5\times$ improvement with non-overlapping intervals
(Figure~\ref{fig:actor}). On antmaze-medium it reaches $0.222$ $[0.188,0.261]$ against a
control of $0.090$ $[0.068,0.118]$. Two results temper this. First, the actor-free
ablation---the same representations acting by per-step latent argmax---scores $0.055$
$[0.047,0.064]$, \emph{below} the behavior-cloning control: $\psi$ is a usable training
signal for the actor but not a reliable ranker of latents, which is the same weakness
Finding 3 diagnosed, surviving the redesign. Second, per-task FQL reaches $0.949$
$[0.936,0.960]$ on the same task, so LatentFlowPSM recovers about a quarter of what a
reward-supervised learner obtains, and its curve is flat from $50$k steps---it converges to
that level rather than being budget-limited.

\label{sec:conclusion}

typicality by construction. Its generative substrate is validated; its open problem---
grounding the policy-index axis of the successor measure against realized behavior---is
